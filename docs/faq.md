# Frequently asked questions

<p class="answer" markdown>Short answers to the questions people ask most about Supportive Stranger. Each answer links to the page with the full details.</p>

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Supportive Stranger?

Supportive Stranger is an iPhone app for short reflection. You write up to 100 words about what is on your mind, an anonymous AI stranger replies kindly and turns your words into an illustration, and for heavy thoughts you write a message to yourself and shatter the image to let it go. See [Home](index.md).

### Is Supportive Stranger available for Android, iPad or the web?

No. Supportive Stranger is an iPhone app, available on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/de/app/supportive-stranger/id6760293353?l=en-GB).

### How much does Supportive Stranger cost?

The download is free and includes one free reflection. A Reflection Package gives you 30 reflections valid for 60 days, for example €14.99 in Germany. There is no subscription. See [Pricing](pricing.md).

### What counts as a reflection?

One reply from the stranger plus one illustration for an entry. Getting a writing prompt, saving entries without AI, and reading your journal never use a reflection.

### Can I use the app without paying?

Yes. You get one free reflection, and you can always write and save entries in your journal for free – they just won't have a reply or illustration without reflections.

### Do I need an account?

No. There is no account, sign-up, email or password.

### Where is my journal stored?

Only on your iPhone. It is not synced or uploaded. Deleting the app deletes your journal. See [Privacy](privacy.md).

### Who reads my entries?

No person. When you send an entry, its text is processed by AI to create the reply and illustration, and it is not kept afterwards.

### Can I lock the app?

Yes. Turn on **Face ID / Touch ID** in [Settings](features/settings.md). The app then asks for Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode each time you open it.

### What is the difference between Dark thoughts and Neutral notes?

*Dark thoughts – Reflect & release* ends with writing to yourself and shattering the image. *Neutral notes – Save & keep* simply saves the reply and image. See [Modes](features/modes.md).

### How do I release a thought?

In *Dark thoughts* mode, write what you would tell yourself (about 30 characters), then tap the image once to crack it and again to shatter it. See [Reflect and release](features/release.md).

### Is my entry deleted when I release the image?

No. Your words – the entry, the stranger's reply and your own reflection – are saved in your journal when you tap **Save entry**.

### What if I don't know what to write?

Tap **Don't know where to start?**, choose when it's from, what it's about and how it feels, and the stranger suggests a prompt. See [Let's sort it out](features/sort-it-out.md).

### What is the 100-word limit for?

To make starting easy and keep you close to the feeling. One honest sentence is enough.

### Is the stranger a real person?

No. The stranger is an AI. It replies kindly, does not judge or advise, and has no memory of you between reflections. See [The stranger's reply](features/the-stranger.md).

### Can I save or share the illustration?

Yes. Tap the button in the corner of the image to open the iOS share sheet, where you can save it to Photos or send it. See [The illustration](features/the-illustration.md).

### Can I change the figure in my illustrations?

Yes. Open Settings and tap **Change Character**.

### How do I search or filter my journal?

Tap the funnel icon in the journal to search, sort by date and filter by time period. Use the slider to show Released or Kept entries. See [The journal](features/journal.md).

### How do I delete entries?

Tap **×** on an entry to delete it, or tap the trash button to select several or all entries and delete them together.

### Does Supportive Stranger have dark mode?

Yes. Choose Light, Auto or Dark in Settings → Appearance.

### How long are purchased reflections valid?

60 days from the date of purchase. Unused reflections then expire.

### Can I buy more reflections before my pack runs out?

No. You can hold one pack at a time. Buy again once your reflections are used up or expired.

### Do reflections renew automatically?

No. The Reflection Package is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

### How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com). See [Withdrawal and refunds](withdrawal.md).

### How do I withdraw from a purchase?

Open Settings and tap **Withdraw from contract here**, fill in your name and email and tap **Confirm withdrawal**. See [Withdrawal and refunds](withdrawal.md).

### Is Supportive Stranger therapy?

No. It is a wellness tool, not a medical device, therapy or crisis service. If you need help now, contact emergency services or a crisis line. See [Safety and wellbeing](safety.md).

### How old do I have to be?

17 or older. Users between 17 and 18 need permission from a parent or guardian.

### What languages does the app support?

English.

### How do I contact Supportive Stranger?

See [Contact and legal](contact.md).
