# Pricing and reflections

<p class="answer" markdown>**How much does Supportive Stranger cost?** The app is free to download and includes **one free reflection**. After that, you can buy a **Reflection Package: 30 reflections, valid for 60 days**, as a one-time in-app purchase through the App Store – for example **€14.99** in Germany (the App Store shows the price in your currency). There is **no subscription** and no automatic renewal. Writing and saving your own entries is always free.</p>

## What is a reflection?

A **reflection** is one AI reply from the stranger **plus** one illustration for an entry. One reflection is used per entry, in either mode (*Dark thoughts* or *Neutral notes*).

| Action | Uses a reflection? |
|---|---|
| Sending an entry with **Talk to Me** and receiving the reply and illustration | **Yes, one** |
| Getting a writing prompt on *Let's sort it out* (including *Try something different*) | No |
| Writing and saving an entry while you have no reflections | No |
| Reading, searching or deleting journal entries | No |
| A reflection where the reply or the illustration failed | No – it is only counted once both arrived |

## The free reflection

Each device gets **one free reflection** when the app is first installed. It always uses the full *Reflect & release* path, so you experience the whole ritual. Reinstalling the app on the same device does not give another free reflection.

## The Reflection Package

| | |
|---|---|
| **Contents** | 30 reflections |
| **Validity** | 60 days from the date of purchase |
| **Price** | Shown in the App Store in your local currency; for example €14.99 in Germany. Apple may add taxes depending on your country. |
| **Type** | Consumable, one-time in-app purchase. Not a subscription; nothing renews automatically. |
| **One pack at a time** | You can buy a new pack only after your current reflections are used up or have expired. |
| **Seller** | Apple Inc. is the merchant of record. |

Unused reflections expire 60 days after purchase and have no further value. Your balance and expiry are shown in [Settings](features/settings.md) (*Expires in N days*). When three or fewer are left, the writing screen reminds you in amber.

## When reflections run out

You don't lose access to your journal. The writing screen says *No reflections left — your entry will be saved locally*. You can keep writing and saving entries; they just won't get a reply or illustration. **Don't know where to start?** is hidden until you buy a new pack.

## Why not a subscription?

Supportive Stranger is designed for moments when you need it, not as a daily habit to keep up. A pack you use at your own pace fits that better than a monthly charge – and the app never tries to keep you coming back.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Supportive Stranger free?

The download is free and includes one free reflection. Further reflections come in packs of 30 that are valid for 60 days. Writing and saving entries without AI is always free.

### How much is a Reflection Package?

It contains 30 reflections valid for 60 days. The price is shown in the App Store in your currency, for example €14.99 in Germany.

### Is Supportive Stranger a subscription?

No. The Reflection Package is a one-time purchase. Nothing renews automatically and there is nothing to cancel.

### What happens to unused reflections after 60 days?

They expire. The expiry date is shown in Settings while your pack is active.

### Can I buy two packs at once?

No. You can hold one pack at a time. The **Buy** button becomes active again once your reflections are used up or expired.

### Do I lose a reflection if the app fails to answer?

No. A reflection is only counted after both the reply and the illustration have been delivered.

### Can I restore my purchase on a new device?

Reflection Packages are consumable purchases, which Apple does not restore. Reflections are linked to the device you bought them on.

### How do I get a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com). See [Withdrawal and refunds](withdrawal.md).
