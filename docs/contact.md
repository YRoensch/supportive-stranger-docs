# Contact and legal

<p class="answer" markdown>**How do I contact Supportive Stranger?** The provider's contact details are in the app under **Settings → Legal Notice** and on [supportive-stranger-app.com](https://supportive-stranger-app.com). For App Store billing and refunds, use [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).</p>

## Legal documents

All legal documents are available in the app under **Settings**:

- **Privacy Policy** – what data is processed, why, and your rights. Also online at [supportive-stranger-app.com](https://supportive-stranger-app.com/#privacy-policy-app).
- **Terms of Service** – eligibility (17+), the free reflection, Reflection Packages, the right of withdrawal and its expiry (section 12), and limitations.
- **Legal Notice** – the provider's name, address and contact details.

If anything on this help site differs from those documents, the documents in the app apply.

## Withdrawing from a purchase

Use **Settings → Withdraw from contract here**. See [Withdrawal and refunds](withdrawal.md).

## Frequently asked questions

### Where can I find the Supportive Stranger Privacy Policy?

In the app under Settings → Privacy Policy, and online at [supportive-stranger-app.com/#privacy-policy-app](https://supportive-stranger-app.com/#privacy-policy-app).

### Who makes Supportive Stranger?

The provider's name and address are listed in the Legal Notice in the app (Settings → Legal Notice).

### Where do I report a problem with a payment?

At [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com), because Apple processes all purchases.
