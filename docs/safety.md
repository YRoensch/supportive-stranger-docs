# Safety and wellbeing

<p class="answer" markdown>**Is Supportive Stranger a replacement for therapy?** No. Supportive Stranger is a wellness tool for a few minutes of reflection and relief. It is not a medical device, not therapy or counselling, not a crisis service, and nobody monitors what you write. If you are in danger or thinking about harming yourself, contact your local emergency number or a crisis line now.</p>

!!! warning "If you need help right now"
    - **Emergency:** call your local emergency number (112 in the EU, 999 in the UK, 911 in the US) or go to the nearest emergency room.
    - **Germany:** TelefonSeelsorge 0800 111 0 111 or 0800 111 0 222 (24/7, free). Young people: 116 111.
    - **UK:** Samaritans 116 123 (24/7, free).
    - **US:** 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline – call or text 988.
    - **Canada:** 1-833-456-4566 (24/7).
    - **Elsewhere:** look up a local crisis line, or contact your doctor.

## What Supportive Stranger is

- A short, self-contained ritual: write, be met, release.
- A way to put a vague, pressing feeling into words and look at it from the outside.
- A private journal for moments you want to let go of or keep.

## What it is not

- Not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition.
- Not therapy, counselling or coaching.
- Not a crisis or emergency service.
- Not a companion or a substitute for human connection. The stranger has no memory of you and the app does not try to keep you engaged.
- Not monitored. No person reads your entries.

## About the AI

The replies and illustrations are created by AI. They can occasionally be inaccurate, miss the point, or feel unexpected. Use your own judgement, and don't base important decisions on them.

## Who can use the app

Supportive Stranger is for people aged **17 and over**. Users between 17 and 18 (or the age of majority where they live) need permission from a parent or guardian.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can Supportive Stranger help in a mental health crisis?

No. It is not a crisis service and nobody monitors entries. Please contact emergency services or a crisis line such as 988 (US), 116 123 (UK) or 0800 111 0 111 (Germany).

### Is Supportive Stranger a medical app?

No. It is a wellness tool for reflection. It does not diagnose, treat or provide clinical services.

### Does anyone read what I write?

No person reads your entries. The text of an entry you send is processed by AI to create the reply and illustration.

### What is the minimum age?

17 years. Users between 17 and 18 need a parent's or guardian's permission.
