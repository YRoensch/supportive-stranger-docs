# Privacy and your data

<p class="answer" markdown>**Is Supportive Stranger private?** Yes, by design. There is no account, no sign-up, no advertising, no tracking and no analytics. Your journal is stored only on your iPhone. When you request a reflection, the text of that entry is processed by AI services in real time to write the reply and create the illustration, and it is not kept afterwards. You can add a Face ID or Touch ID lock. The full details are in the [Privacy Policy](https://supportive-stranger-app.com/#privacy-policy-app).</p>

## Anonymous from the start

Anonymity is not an extra; it's part of how the app works. You can be honest because there is no profile to protect.

- **No account** – no name, email or password is ever asked for during normal use.
- **No identity** – the app uses a random, anonymous identifier to keep track of your reflection balance. It isn't linked to your name or Apple Account details.
- **No ads, trackers or analytics.**
- The only place you can choose to share your name and email is the [withdrawal form](withdrawal.md).

## What stays on your iPhone

- Your **journal**: everything you write, the stranger's replies, the illustrations and your own reflections.
- Your **settings**: character, appearance, Face ID lock.

Because your journal lives only on your device:

- Deleting the app, resetting your iPhone or losing it can permanently delete your entries and any unused reflections.
- Nobody can recover or transfer your journal for you.
- If you want to keep your entries, use your iPhone's own backup (for example iCloud Backup) and don't rely on the app as a permanent archive.

## What happens when you request a reflection

When you tap **Talk to Me** with reflections available, the text of that one entry and the character you selected are processed by third-party AI services to write the stranger's reply and create the illustration. They are processed in real time and are not retained afterwards. Your other journal entries are never sent. The options on *Let's sort it out* are used only to write a prompt.

Entries saved without a reflection are not sent anywhere.

## Locking the app

Switch on **Face ID / Touch ID** in [Settings](features/settings.md). The app then locks whenever you leave it and asks for Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode when you return.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does Supportive Stranger read my journal?

No. Your journal is stored only on your iPhone. Only the text of an entry you send with **Talk to Me** is processed by AI to create that reflection, and it is not kept afterwards.

### Is my data used to train AI?

Your entries are processed only to create your reflection and are not retained afterwards. See the [Privacy Policy](https://supportive-stranger-app.com/#privacy-policy-app) for the details about the AI providers.

### Does the app track me or show ads?

No. There are no ads, no advertising trackers, no cookies and no analytics.

### Can someone else open my journal on my phone?

Turn on **Face ID / Touch ID** in Settings. The app will then require Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode every time it is opened.

### What happens to my entries if I delete the app?

They are deleted with the app, because they are stored only on your device. Unused reflections may be lost as well.

### Where is the full Privacy Policy?

In the app under **Settings → Privacy Policy**, and online at [supportive-stranger-app.com](https://supportive-stranger-app.com/#privacy-policy-app).
